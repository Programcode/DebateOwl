Start with burdens

Before writing contentions, define what your side must prove. A case that misses the burden can sound polished and still lose.

Write contentions as reasoning chains

Each contention should connect claim, warrant, evidence, impact, and weighing. If one piece is missing, mark it before you practice.

Pressure-test the draft

Use DebateLab to attack your own case. The strongest final version usually comes after finding the easiest rebuttals first.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
