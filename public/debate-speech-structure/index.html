Why structure matters

A well-structured speech is easier to flow, easier to judge, and easier to win. The judge should never be confused about where you are.

Opening speech structure

State your position, define key terms, present contentions with warrants and impacts, and preview weighing.

Rebuttal and final speech structure

Respond to the key clash, extend your offense, weigh the comparison, and frame the ballot. End by telling the judge why you win.
