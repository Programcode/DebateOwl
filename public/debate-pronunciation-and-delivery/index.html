Why delivery matters

The judge cannot vote for an argument they did not hear clearly. Speed without clarity is wasted effort.

How to improve delivery

Slow down on tags and author names, pause between contentions, and emphasize weighing phrases. Practice with a timer and record yourself.

Common delivery problems

Speaking too fast, mumbling, monotone delivery, and forgetting to pause. Each of these can cost you rounds even with strong arguments.
