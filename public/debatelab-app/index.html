What the DebateLab app includes

The app includes structured debate tools for generating cases, attacking weak arguments, researching evidence leads, mapping flowcharts, asking coaching questions, and timing speeches.

Public pages vs private app

Public pages explain what DebateLab does and help searchers find the product. The app itself protects saved cases, account data, shared links, and user-specific histories from public indexing.

When to open the app

Open the app when you have a motion, speech, case, or argument you want to improve. Use the public guides when you want to understand a debate workflow before practicing.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
