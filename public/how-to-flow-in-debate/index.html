Why flowing matters

If you cannot see the argument structure, you cannot respond strategically. Flowing keeps the full round visible on one page.

How to flow efficiently

Use columns for each speech, abbreviate claims and warrants, track the flow of arguments across speeches, and mark dropped arguments.

Using your flow in speeches

Before you speak, look at the flow and decide which clashes you must win. Your speech should trace the flow, not introduce random new arguments.
