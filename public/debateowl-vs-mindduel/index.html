What is the difference between DebateOwl and MindDuel?

They train different things. MindDuel, an Android app, gives you ten short spoken turns against an AI, each 5 to 60 seconds, then a 0–100 score across five areas. DebateOwl prepares a full round: a case you can read out, "If they say… we answer…" replies, and a timed practice round at your format's real speech times, ending in a verdict. Use MindDuel for quick reflexes on your phone, and DebateOwl when a motion and a date are set.

The motion for this page

"Public transport in cities should be free to ride."

Below, the same motion goes through both apps, step by step. In MindDuel that works if one of its topics is this motion or close to it; whether you can type your own is not stated.

Two workflows, side by side

In MindDuel

  1. Set up. Choose a topic (its listing names politics, technology, ethics and society) and a level: Beginner, Intermediate or Expert. Set thinking time and speaking time, each 5 to 60 seconds.
  2. Debate. Ten rounds, you against the AI, spoken through voice-to-text.
  3. Result. A 0–100 total, scored on clarity, logic and structure, evidence and examples, persuasiveness and rebuttal, with a short explanation.
  4. Afterwards. The debate is saved to your history, and your score can put you on a global leaderboard.

In DebateOwl

  1. Set up. Open a Current Debate with the motion as it will be announced, and pick your side.
  2. Prepare. Generate the case: opening, two to four arguments with evidence and why they matter, "If they say… we answer…" replies, closing line, sources. Open the sources.
  3. Debate. Run Sparring in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum at the real speech times, typed or spoken. Drill questions in Crossfire if you like.
  4. Result. A judged verdict that names the reason. Rebuild that one part and run the speech again.

MindDuel trains how fast you can answer. DebateOwl trains what you say when the round is real. Neither replaces the other.

What the DebateOwl case looks like for this motion

Opening: A bus that is free to board is a bus more people use, and a city where more people take the bus is easier to live in for everyone, drivers included.

Argument 1, cleaner, quicker streets: fewer car trips means less traffic and less exhaust in the centre. Evidence: find a study of a city that dropped fares, with ridership before and after; note the year. Why it matters: every bus seat filled is a car not queuing.

Argument 2, fairness: fares take the biggest share from people on the lowest wages. Evidence: find household spending data on transport by income. Why it matters: free fares are a raise for the people who need one most.

If they say the city cannot afford it, we answer: fare collection has its own costs, and the city already pays for congestion in lost time and road repairs.

If they say free buses will be overcrowded, we answer: crowding is a reason to run more buses, and that is a better problem than empty ones.

Closing: Make the bus the easy choice, and the city gets moving.

The two "If they say…" lines make good MindDuel material: each is a 30-second answer you can practise saying without notes. That is one honest way to use both.

Pick by what you have this week

  • Ten spare minutes and a phone: MindDuel. Short turns are the point.
  • A motion and a tournament date: DebateOwl. You need the case before the reflexes.
  • Nerves when you speak: start with MindDuel on Beginner, then move to full timed speeches in DebateOwl.
  • A Public Forum or Lincoln-Douglas round: DebateOwl, because Sparring uses those formats' speech order and times.

MindDuel questions

Which MindDuel is this page about?
The Android app "MindDuel – AI Debate Practice" by Serdar Yalkabov on Google Play. We found no website for it. mindduel.io is an unrelated puzzle game.
How much does MindDuel cost?
As of October 2026 its Google Play listing shows in-app purchases but does not state prices.
Can I enter my own motion in MindDuel?
Not stated. Its listing describes real-world topics in politics, technology, ethics and society.
Does DebateOwl score me out of 100?
No. A Sparring round ends with a judged verdict that names the reason you won or lost, which you can turn into the next fix.
Can I speak instead of type in both?
Yes. MindDuel uses voice-to-text. DebateOwl's Sparring speeches can be typed or spoken.
