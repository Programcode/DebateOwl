Why weighing wins rounds

If both sides have offense, the judge needs a reason to prefer yours. Weighing gives them that reason by comparing impacts across multiple dimensions.

Types of weighing

Magnitude: how big is the impact? Probability: how likely is it? Timeframe: how soon? Reversibility: can it be undone? Scope: who is affected? Link strength: how certain is the causal chain?

When to weigh

Start weighing in the constructive, reinforce in rebuttals, and crystallize in the final speech. Late weighing is better than no weighing, but early weighing frames the entire round.
