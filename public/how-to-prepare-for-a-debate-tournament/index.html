Topic research workflow

Start with topic analysis, identify key clashes, research both sides, and build case templates. DebateLab helps you move from topic to structured prep.

Building block files

Block files are pre-written responses to common arguments. Use DebateLab to brainstorm likely arguments and prepare blocks before the tournament.

Tournament day checklist

Bring extra pens, backup evidence, water, snacks, a timer, and a plan for each round. Mental stamina matters as much as preparation.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
