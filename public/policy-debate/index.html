How policy debate works

Policy debate asks whether a plan should be adopted. Prep involves plans, advantages, disadvantages, counterplans, topicality, and kritiks.

DebateLab helps you draft and attack each component.

Researching DAs and counterplans

Use DebateLab to find potential disadvantages, test counterplan competition, and build blocks for common DA scenarios.

Kritik and theory prep

Kritiks challenge assumptions behind the opponent's framework. DebateLab can help you brainstorm kritik arguments and theory blocks.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
