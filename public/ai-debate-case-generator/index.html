What an AI case generator should do

A good case generator does not write your case for you. It gives you structure: claims, warrants, impacts, and research leads that you verify and adapt.

Citation and evidence workflows

Generated evidence leads are starting points, not final cards. Always check the original source, confirm the quote, and make sure the evidence supports your link.

How DebateLab's case generator works

DebateLab generates structured draft cases that you can attack, refine, and prepare. The private workspace holds your saved cases and history.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
