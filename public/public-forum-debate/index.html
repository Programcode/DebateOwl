How PF cases work

A PF case has two contentions, each with a claim, warrant, impact, and evidence. The strongest cases also include pre-emption and weighing from the constructive.

DebateLab helps you draft, attack, and refine PF cases before the round.

Crossfire and rebuttal practice

Crossfire exposes weak warrants and missing evidence. Use DebateLab to generate likely crossfire questions and practice ten-second rebuttal blocks.

Summary and final focus

The best summaries collapse to the winning argument. Practice selecting the right clash and framing the ballot for the judge.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
