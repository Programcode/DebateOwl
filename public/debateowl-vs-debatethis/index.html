Should a new debater start with DebateThis or DebateOwl?

Start with DebateThis if you want to try debating as a game: free one-on-one matches of three timed rounds (opening, rebuttal, closing) against people or AI bots, with an Elo rating that moves after every win or loss. Start with DebateOwl if a school or club debate is coming and you want a coach: it writes your case with "If they say… we answer…" replies, questions you on it, and runs a timed round in your format that ends with what to work on next.

A ladder and a coach

The ladder: DebateThis

An online arena where every match is one-on-one and has the same three phases. Win and your Elo rating climbs, through tiers from Unranked to Senator; lose and it drops. When nobody is around, eight house bots with distinct styles (formal, aggressive, thoughtful, snarky) will take the other side at any hour. Spectators vote on who argued better. T1 T4

The coach: DebateOwl

A prep and practice app built around one motion at a time. It writes the case you will deliver, questions you on it in Crossfire, and runs a practice round at the real times of your format. At the end a judge's verdict says what worked and what to fix before the next round.

One is built to keep you coming back for another match. The other is built to get you ready for a particular round. A new debater can use both, but most will want to start in one.

Four questions before your first debate

Question 1

I have never debated. Which should I open first?

If you just want to find out whether arguing is fun, DebateThis: it is free to play and you can watch its bots debate before you sign up. If your first debate is already on the calendar, DebateOwl, because it starts from that motion and builds the case you will say.

Start with DebateThis

Question 2

Which one tells me what I did wrong?

DebateOwl. Each Sparring round ends with a judge's verdict, a score per category and what to work on next. On DebateThis, your rating and the audience votes tell you whether you won; the pages we read describe no written notes on your arguments.

Question 3

Can I practise a school motion in a real format?

DebateOwl takes any motion and runs School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum at their real speech times. DebateThis has its own three-round match and a topic catalog from pop culture to philosophy; its pages name no school format.

What does it cost to start?

DebateThis says it is free to play, and its pages show no paid plan. DebateOwl's app is $5 a month after a 3-day free trial, while its timer, motion generator and flow sheet cost nothing.

A first week with both

  1. Day 1. Watch a match in DebateThis's Bot Arena; no account needed to spectate. Notice how each side answers the other in the rebuttal phase. T2
  2. Day 2. Play a free DebateThis match against a house bot on a topic you care about.
  3. Day 3. Put your club's motion into DebateOwl and read the case it writes, out loud.
  4. Days 4 to 5. Answer Crossfire's questions on that case, then run one Sparring round and read the verdict.
  5. The weekend. Back on the ladder with what you learned, or straight into the real debate.

DebateThis's leaderboard is open to read, and its human ranking shows how many matches the top players have won and lost. T3
