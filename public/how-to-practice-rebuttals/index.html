How to Practice Debate Rebuttals

Build sharper responses by attacking warrants, evidence, impacts, and weighing one drill at a time.

Do not rebut everything

Good rebuttal practice teaches selection. Answer the argument that determines the round, not every sentence your opponent says.

Use repeatable drills

Run separate drills for weak warrants, missing examples, evidence quality, impact defense, and comparative weighing.

Turn attacks into speeches

After finding objections, practice explaining them under time. The goal is a clean speech, not a long list of notes.
