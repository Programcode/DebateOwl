Two AI debate prep platforms compared on features and workflows.

Which is better, DebateLab or WinDebate?

It depends on your format, budget and how you practise. As of October 2026, WinDebate names six formats including Congressional, Policy and British Parliamentary, has a free tier with monthly limits, and adds live-round capture, replay review and progress tracking, with paid plans from $19.99/month. DebateLab costs $5/month + tax after a 3-day trial, runs full practice rounds against an AI opponent in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum, and works in English, Spanish and Hungarian. Choose WinDebate for Policy, Congress or season tracking, and DebateLab for low-cost case building and sparring.

Formats

As of October 2026, WinDebate names six formats: Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Congressional, Policy, World Schools and British Parliamentary. DebateLab's Sparring rounds cover School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum.

Price

WinDebate has a free tier with monthly limits and paid plans from $19.99/month. DebateLab has a 3-day free trial, then Pro at $5/month + tax.

Workflow

WinDebate is organised as prep, practice, live round and review. DebateLab keeps cases, sources, practice rounds and debriefs together in one Current Debate.

What WinDebate offers

WinDebate (windebate.ai) describes itself as a debate training platform with four stages: prep, practice, live and review. Its site lists research on both sides with evidence and rebuttal trees, targeted drills for attack, defense, weighing and speeches, a Live Round Assistant that captures speeches and builds response cards, replay review that turns a round into drills, and progress tracking.

As of October 2026, its plans are Free ($0, with 1 research session, 10 drills and 1 Live Assistant round a month), Plus ($19.99/month, which adds voice input and playback and weekly progress) and Pro ($39.99/month). These details come from WinDebate's own site; check it for current terms.

What DebateLab offers

DebateLab is an AI debate prep and practice app with 8 prep modes. You start a Current Debate for one motion, add sources to its Library, generate a structured case with a predicted opponent case, find weak links with Attack, research claims with Factoids, ask the Ask coach, drill cross-examination in Crossfire, and run timed Sparring rounds against an AI opponent that end in a verdict. A speech timer and an argument flowchart round it out.

As of October 2026, DebateLab has a 3-day free trial with no credit card, then Pro at $5/month + tax. The app is available in English, Spanish and Hungarian.

How to choose

  • You debate Policy, Congress or British Parliamentary and want a tool that names your format: WinDebate lists all three.
  • You want to track progress across a season or review recorded rounds: WinDebate lists progress tracking and replay review.
  • You want a free tier you can keep using: WinDebate has one; DebateLab does not.
  • You want full practice rounds against an AI opponent in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum, with a verdict: DebateLab.
  • You prepare in Spanish or Hungarian, or want the lowest paid price: DebateLab.

The fairest test is to run the same motion through both during DebateLab's trial and WinDebate's free tier, then compare the cases, the feedback and how much setup each needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab cheaper than WinDebate?

As of October 2026, DebateLab Pro is $5/month + tax and WinDebate's paid plans start at $19.99/month. WinDebate also has a free tier with monthly limits; DebateLab has a 3-day trial and no permanent free plan.

Which formats does each tool support?

WinDebate names Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Congressional, Policy, World Schools and British Parliamentary. DebateLab's case, Attack, Ask and Crossfire tools work for any motion, and Sparring runs School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum rounds.

Can I use both?

Yes. They are separate products, and some debaters use one for prep and another for review.
