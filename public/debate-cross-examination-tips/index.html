How to design cross-ex questions

Ask leading, closed questions that force yes or no answers. Never give the opponent a platform to make new arguments.

Time management in cross-ex

Prioritize the questions that expose the biggest weakness. Do not waste time on minor points that will not affect the ballot.

Using concessions in your speech

If the opponent concedes a point in cross-ex, remind the judge in your speech. Concessions are the strongest form of evidence.
