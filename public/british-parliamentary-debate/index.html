BP roles and strategy

BP has four teams: Opening Government, Opening Opposition, Closing Government, Closing Opposition. Each role has different responsibilities and winning strategies.

Extensions that win

Closing teams must extend by adding new analysis, not repeating opening arguments. DebateLab helps you brainstorm credible extensions from any motion.

POIs and weighing

Points of information expose holes in reasoning. Practice generating and answering POIs, and compare impacts clearly for the judge.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
