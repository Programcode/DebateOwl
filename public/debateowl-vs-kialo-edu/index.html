Should a teacher use Kialo Edu or DebateOwl for debate?

They do different jobs, so a teacher can use both. Kialo Edu is a free, nonprofit discussion platform where a class maps an argument: a thesis with pro and con claims under it, which students add sources to, vote on and get graded on. DebateOwl gets students ready to say a case aloud: it writes the case with "If they say… we answer…" replies and runs timed practice rounds. Map the motion in Kialo Edu, then debate it in the room with DebateOwl's free timer and flow sheet.

Mapping an argument vs preparing to speak it

Kialo Edu answers "what are all the reasons, and how do they connect?" DebateOwl answers "what will I say in four minutes, and what will I say back?" As of November 2026, from Kialo Edu's own pages and DebateOwl's app:

One 50-minute lesson that uses both

For a class of any size, no student accounts anywhere. Kialo Edu's own FAQ puts sign-up to a running discussion at under two minutes. K3

  1. 0–5 min

    Open a starter discussion from Kialo Edu's Topic Library, or type the motion as the thesis. Turn on Instant Access and put the QR code on the board.

  2. 5–20 min

    Small Group Mode gives each group its own copy. Groups add pro and con claims under the thesis, each with a source. You cycle through the copies and leave comments.

  3. 20–25 min

    Guided Voting walks each group through its map; the two strongest claims on each side become tonight's arguments.

  4. 25–45 min

    Close the laptops. Two speakers a side debate the motion in the room, timed on DebateOwl's free debate timer, while everyone else fills in a printed flow sheet.

  5. 45–50 min

    Back to the map: which claims survived the debate, and which reply nobody had written down? Export the discussion as plain text for next lesson.

The tools for minutes 25 to 45: the debate timer has presets for Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, British Parliamentary, World Schools, Asian Parliamentary and School 4-6-6-3, and the flow sheet can be printed for every student.

Homework for the debate club

Students who compete take the strongest claims from the map into DebateOwl. The case generator turns them into a speech with evidence and replies, Crossfire questions them on it, and Sparring runs a timed round that ends with what to fix.

Teachers ask

Is Kialo Edu really free?
Yes. As of November 2026 Kialo Edu says it is free and always will be, with no premium features, because Kialo is a nonprofit funded by a private philanthropic foundation.
Do students need accounts?
Not for either tool's free part. Kialo Edu's Instant Access lets students join a discussion by link or QR code with only a name, and DebateOwl's timer and flow sheet need no sign-in. Teachers need a Kialo Edu account, and students need their own account for the DebateOwl app.
Does Kialo Edu use AI?
The Kialo Edu pages we read describe argument maps, voting, sources, tasks and grading, and none of them mention AI. It is a tool for students' own reasoning, not an AI opponent or case writer.
