AI research is a starting point

AI can suggest where to look: which journals, news sources, and reports might support your link. But you must open and verify the actual source.

How to verify evidence

Check author credentials, publication date, methodology, and whether the quote is in context. A card that misrepresents the source will hurt you in cross-ex.

Linking evidence to arguments

Evidence is not self-explanatory. You must explain how it supports your warrant and impact. DebateLab helps connect research to clash.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
