Which is better, DebateLab or DebateClub?

They solve different problems. As of October 2026, DebateClub is voice-only sparring for high-stakes conversations such as negotiations, pitches and meetings, with opponent personas and rhetorical technique scoring, and Pro costs $19/month. It names no competitive debate formats. DebateLab is built for school and competitive debate: cases, rebuttals, evidence, a timer and timed rounds in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum, at $5/month + tax after a 3-day trial. Choose DebateClub for workplace persuasion practice and DebateLab for tournament prep.

Audience

As of October 2026, DebateClub describes itself as training for high-stakes conversations such as negotiations, pitches and meetings. DebateLab is built for school and competitive debate.

Practice style

DebateClub is voice-only sparring with opponent personas. DebateLab combines case prep tools with timed Sparring rounds you can type or speak.

Price

DebateClub has a free tier with one debate credit and Pro at $19/month. DebateLab has a 3-day free trial, then Pro at $5/month + tax.

What DebateClub offers

DebateClub (debateclub.app) calls itself a training platform for high-stakes conversations: you speak out loud and an AI sparring partner responds in real time. Its site lists opponent personas such as a skeptical investor or a hostile critic, detection and scoring of rhetorical techniques, session recordings and a prep screen for openings and counters. It does not name school or competitive debate formats.

As of October 2026, its plans are Free ($0, one debate credit) and Pro ($19/month or $149/year). These details come from DebateClub's own site; check it for current terms.

What DebateLab offers

DebateLab is an AI debate prep and practice app for students and competitive debaters, with 11 prep modes. It builds structured cases with a predicted opponent case, finds weak links with Attack, researches claims with Factoids, drills cross-examination in Crossfire and runs timed Sparring rounds in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum that end in a verdict. Voice mode lets you speak your speeches instead of typing them.

As of October 2026, DebateLab has a 3-day free trial with no credit card, then Pro at $5/month + tax. The app is available in English, Spanish and Hungarian.

Which fits you

  • Preparing for a negotiation, pitch, interview or meeting: DebateClub is built for that.
  • Practising out loud against interruptions and difficult personas: DebateClub.
  • Preparing cases, rebuttals and evidence for a debate tournament: DebateLab.
  • Practising full rounds in a school debate format with speech times and a verdict: DebateLab.
