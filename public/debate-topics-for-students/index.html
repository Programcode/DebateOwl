How to pick a debate topic

A good topic has clear clashes, real-world stakes, and enough literature for both sides. DebateLab can help you brainstorm cases from any topic.

Popular debate topics

Common topic areas include technology, education, environment, foreign policy, civil liberties, economics, and ethics. Each area has predictable clashes.

From topic to prep

Once you pick a topic, use DebateLab to draft a case, attack it, find evidence leads, and practice the speech.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
