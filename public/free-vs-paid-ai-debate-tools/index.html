What $0 gets you

  • ChatGPT: a free version OpenAI says is available to everyone, including study mode and voice, with limits.
  • Claude: a $0 plan with web search, memory and voice mode.
  • DebateOwl's separate tools: a timer, motion generator, printable flow sheet and speech-time calculator, no account. The app itself is a 3-day trial, not a permanent free plan.
  • PublicForumAI: 3 full practice rounds a month, unlimited AI Flow drills, 5 cases a month plus 2 with real sources, and 15 training modules.
  • Debate Ladder: 100 credits for a new account, about one credit per minute of speaking.
  • ArguFight: free to start, free forever for base debating, and free for classrooms.
  • Debatable: 10 AI voice minutes after sign-in, not refilled, and live rounds with people at no charge.
  • Cardinal: the core tools, five of them marked free, described as free forever.
  • HyperWrite: a limited trial of the debate assistant.
  • Debate Arena: a free plan with unlimited debates against AI and live opponents.
  • Symbai: a trial, then a read-only workspace if you do not pay.
  • Kialo Edu: the classroom product, free, and the site says it always will be.
  • Prilo's pages we opened do not state a free option.

What paying adds

Across these pages, payment tends to buy the same kinds of things, and the lists above are the ones to trust:

  • More rounds or more minutes, once a free cap runs out.
  • Sourced evidence with links, where the product says the free tier argues without them.
  • Voice time, on the products that meter speaking.
  • Teacher dashboards, student analytics or class controls, on the products built for a whole class.

DebateOwl's $5 plan is the low monthly price that adds the prep workspace: all 10 models, Active Grounding research and priority support. Open every source before a lead goes into a speech. Active Grounding is not a promise that a citation is already checked.

  1. Use the free tools for drills: the timer, the flow sheet and the motion generator. No account.
  2. Use a free chatbot for background reading. It will not keep your motion, side and speech times for you.
  3. Buy one month of a paid tool during tournament weeks. DebateOwl is $5/month + tax after the 3-day trial, which is the straightforward way to get the case, the replies and the timed rounds in one place. Price questions

Is there a free AI for debate practice?
Yes. ChatGPT and Claude have free plans, PublicForumAI includes 3 full practice rounds a month, Debate Ladder gives new accounts 100 credits, and ArguFight is free to start. DebateOwl's app is paid after a 3-day trial, and its timer and motion generator cost nothing.
What is the cheapest paid AI debate tool?
Among the tools in this table, the lowest monthly prices listed are Debatable's Tokens plan at $4.99/month, DebateOwl at $5/month + tax, and Symbai's individual plan at NZ$4.99/month. Debatable's Individual plan is $10 a year. Compare what each one actually includes before you treat the smallest number as the same product.
Is DebateOwl free?
The app is not: you get a 3-day free trial, then Pro at $5/month + tax. The timer, flow sheet and motion generator on its free tools page cost nothing.
Are free AI debate tools good enough?
For drills and background reading, often yes. Paid plans mainly add volume and linked evidence. Whatever you use, check evidence against the original source. For a tournament week, DebateOwl at $5/month is the low-priced way to get a case, replies and timed rounds together.
Is ChatGPT free for debate?
Yes. OpenAI says the free version is available to everyone. Paid plans add more access.
