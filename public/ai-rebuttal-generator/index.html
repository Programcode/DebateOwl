What an AI rebuttal generator should do

A useful tool points you to the weakest part of the opponent's argument and helps you build a targeted response, not a generic counter-narrative.

How to use generated rebuttals

Take the generated block, adapt it to the specific argument, and practice saying it under time. The best rebuttals are concise and repeated.

Pair with cross-ex

If your rebuttal attacks a warrant, your cross-ex should force the opponent to defend or abandon that warrant. Practice both with DebateLab.

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.
