Motion interpretation

WSDC motions are broad. Define the model carefully, identify stakeholders, and clarify what each side must prove before writing speeches.

Team roles in WSDC

First speaker sets up, second speaker extends, third speaker weighs and rebuts, reply speaker frames the ballot. Practice each role with DebateLab.

POIs and reply speeches

POIs test warrants under pressure. Reply speeches summarize the round without new arguments. Practice both to improve your WSDC prep.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
