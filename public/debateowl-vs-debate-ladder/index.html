What DebateOwl does well

A DebateOwl case is a speech you can read out: an opening, two to four arguments, each with an explanation, evidence that names its source and year, and why it matters, then "If they say… we answer…" replies, a closing line, and the sources it used. You can regenerate one weak section without touching the rest.

Speaker roles follow a school round. The introductor gets the opening and the headline arguments. The first refutation speaker gets the full arguments. The second speaker brings no new arguments: about two minutes backing up the team's arguments, then about four minutes on "If they say X, we answer Y" replies, usually five or six blocks. The closing speaker brings no new arguments either: two or three reasons your side won, then the closing line.

Around that case, Attack looks for the weak link in the other side, Factoids researches a claim and names sources you still have to open, Crossfire drills questions, and Sparring runs a timed round in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum and ends with a judged verdict. World Schools, British Parliamentary, Policy and Congress use the prep tools, not a full simulated round. The app is in English, Spanish and Hungarian. Pro adds expanded AI prep access, all 10 models (including Claude Opus and Sonnet, GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini), Active Grounding research and priority support. Evidence leads still need checking against the original source. The DebateOwl app has no free plan (3-day free trial, then $5/month); the separate tools at /tools are free with no account.

The free debate timer is separate from the app: seven formats, protected time, points-of-information knocks and prep banks, and it saves the round in the browser. See also the case generator, practice rounds, rebuttal practice and pricing.

What Debate Ladder does well

Debate Ladder is best for people who want frequent out-loud reps and a ranking, on any topic, including non-competitors. Its homepage describes a voice-first debate game. You are assigned PRO or CON, then speak an opening, rebuttal and closing while an AI argues the other side. Practice mode lets you type. An AI judge scores logic, responsiveness, clarity, delivery and persuasiveness, then gives feedback. Ranked wins raise your Elo, which starts at 500, and losses lower it. Practice and custom debates are unrated. Topics run from beginner to expert, and you can write your own.
