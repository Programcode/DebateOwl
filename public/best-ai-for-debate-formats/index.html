Three questions that pick the tool

  1. Does it keep your format's speech order and times? A tool that only offers "opening, rebuttal, closing" will not teach you to collapse a PF summary into three minutes or to hold a BP extension for seven.
  2. Does it cover the part you lose on? If judges keep writing that your questioning gave nothing away, you need a cross-examination drill more than another case. If your closing team keeps repeating opening, you need angles, not a timer.
  3. What does a month cost, and what is free before you pay? Prices differ by a factor of eight across the tools on these pages, and several have free tiers with monthly limits.

Format questions

Which formats can I run a full timed round in?

School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum. A round runs the speeches in order at their real lengths, typed or spoken, against an AI opponent, and ends with a verdict scored on logic, evidence, delivery and strategy.

Does DebateOwl have a free plan?

No. The app starts with a 3-day free trial that asks for no credit card, then costs $5/month plus tax. The debate timer, motion generator, flow sheet and speech-time calculator are free without an account, and so is the one-exchange mini round on the AI debate page.

Why does a format-specific tool matter?

Because the clock shapes the speech. A four-minute PF rebuttal, a seven-minute BP extension and a four-minute World Schools reply ask for different things, and practice at the wrong length trains the wrong habit.

Can I use these tools during a round?

Treat them as preparation. Whether a device may be used in a round, or in BP and World Schools prep time, is your league's rule, so read it before the tournament.
