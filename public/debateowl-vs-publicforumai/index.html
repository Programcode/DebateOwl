Should I use DebateOwl or PublicForumAI?

If you debate more than Public Forum, or switch formats between tournaments, DebateOwl is the one that follows you: timed practice rounds in Public Forum and Lincoln-Douglas, prep tools and a free timer for Policy, British Parliamentary and World Schools, and prep tools for Congress. If Public Forum is the only format you will ever debate, PublicForumAI is built for exactly that, with full practice rounds judged by a tech or lay judge, and its free plan gives you 3 rounds a month.

If Public Forum is all you debate

PublicForumAI was started in 2024 by high school debaters in New York City, and every tool follows the Public Forum speech order. A full round takes about 35 minutes with prep time, you choose a tech or lay judge, and premium rounds argue from real evidence with a link for each card. It asks for a computer or a large screen. Its free plan is generous enough that a PF debater can try a full round before paying anything. P1 P2

DebateOwl's case is the same shape in every format: an opening, two to four arguments with evidence and why each matters, then "If they say… we answer…" replies and a closing. That is what lets one account cover a PF season and a British Parliamentary weekend.

Three short questions

Is PublicForumAI free?
Yes. As of October 2026 its free plan includes 3 full practice rounds and 5 AI cases a month. Premium is $9.99 a month, or $79 a year, for unlimited rounds and linked evidence.
Does either one practise crossfire?
PublicForumAI's site says a crossfire fast enough for a real round is in the works. DebateOwl drills it separately in Crossfire, and its Sparring round runs the speeches without crossfire periods.
I only debate Public Forum. Which should I pick?
On price, PublicForumAI: its free plan already runs full rounds with a ballot. DebateOwl is the pick when you want crossfire drills, replies written into every case, and room to move to Lincoln-Douglas or World Schools later.
