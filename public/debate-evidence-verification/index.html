Why verification matters

If your evidence is challenged and found wanting, your credibility is gone. A verified card is a weapon; an unverified one is a liability.

How to verify evidence

Check the author, publication date, methodology, sample size, and context of the quote. Make sure it actually says what you claim it says.

What to do if evidence is weak

Do not read it. Find a better source, or make the argument analytically without relying on a weak card.
