How to Write a Rebuttal Speech

Select, attack, extend, and frame — the four moves of a winning rebuttal.

Ballot framing

Select the right clash

Do not answer every argument. Identify the clash that decides the round and focus your rebuttal there.

Attack warrants, not just claims

A claim without a warrant is a bare assertion. Attack the link between the claim and the evidence, and you collapse the entire argument.

Frame the ballot

End your rebuttal by telling the judge exactly why your side wins. If you do not give them a story, they will write their own.
