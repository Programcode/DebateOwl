What an AI debate coach should do

The useful coach asks what the round is about, what side you are on, what your burden is, and what the judge needs to compare.

DebateLab supports strategy, structure, weighing, rebuttals, and speech practice rather than generic essay writing.

Use coaching to improve faster

After a practice speech, ask what was unclear, what was under-warranted, and what should become the collapse. Then repeat with a narrower drill.

Public guidance, private workspace

Coaching explanations can be public and indexable. Your saved cases, history, and private prep remain protected behind login.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
