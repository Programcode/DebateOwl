What an AI debate coach should do

The useful coach asks what the round is about, what side you are on, what your burden is, and what the judge needs to compare.

DebateLab is designed to support strategy, structure, weighing, rebuttals, and speech practice rather than generic essay writing.

Use coaching to improve faster

After a practice speech, ask what was unclear, what was under-warranted, and what should become the collapse. Then repeat with a narrower drill.

That loop turns feedback into deliberate practice instead of vague advice.

Public guidance, private workspace

The coaching explanations can be public and indexable. Your saved cases, history, and private prep should remain protected behind login.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI coach replace a human coach?

No. It is a practice tool, not a substitute for a great human coach or team.

What can I ask it?

Strategy, structure, rebuttals, weighing, speech drills, and prep questions.

Is my private prep public?

No. Private app content should stay behind login and out of search.
