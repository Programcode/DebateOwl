Evidence is not the argument

Cards win rounds only when they support a clear warrant. A source should answer a burden, not decorate a speech.

DebateLab helps move from a claim to research leads, source checks, and the impact comparison the judge must evaluate.

Verify before you read it aloud

Open the original source, check date and author, confirm the quote, and explain why the evidence proves your link. Never rely on an AI summary alone.

Connect research to clash

The best evidence pages are tied to specific clashes: solvency, harms, tradeoffs, comparative examples, and weighing.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
