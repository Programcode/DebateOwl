Which is better, DebateOwl or DebateClub?

They solve different problems. As of October 2026, DebateClub is voice-only sparring for high-stakes conversations such as negotiations, pitches and meetings, with opponent personas and rhetorical technique scoring, and Pro costs $19/month. It names no competitive debate formats. DebateOwl is built for school and competitive debate: cases, rebuttals, evidence, a timer and timed rounds in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum, at $5/month + tax after a 3-day trial. Choose DebateClub for workplace persuasion practice and DebateOwl for tournament prep.

What DebateClub offers

DebateClub (debateclub.app) calls itself a training platform for high-stakes conversations: you speak out loud and an AI sparring partner responds in real time. Its site lists opponent personas such as a skeptical investor or a hostile critic, detection and scoring of rhetorical techniques, session recordings and a prep screen for openings and counters. It does not name school or competitive debate formats.

As of October 2026, its plans are Free ($0, one debate credit) and Pro ($19/month or $149/year). These details come from DebateClub's own site; check it for current terms.

What DebateOwl offers

DebateOwl is an AI debate prep and practice app for students and competitive debaters, with 8 prep modes. It builds structured cases with a predicted opponent case, finds weak links with Attack, researches claims with Factoids, drills cross-examination in Crossfire and runs timed Sparring rounds in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum that end in a verdict. Voice mode lets you speak your speeches instead of typing them.

As of October 2026, DebateOwl has a 3-day free trial with no credit card, then Pro at $5/month + tax. The app is available in English, Spanish and Hungarian.
