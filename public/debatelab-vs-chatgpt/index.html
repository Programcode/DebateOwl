What ChatGPT does well

ChatGPT is a flexible general-purpose AI. It can brainstorm, draft, and answer questions about any topic, including debate.

What DebateLab does differently

DebateLab is designed for debate prep workflows: case structure, targeted rebuttals, evidence leads, argument flowcharts, coaching prompts, and timed practice. Each workflow supports a specific prep task.

When to use each

Use ChatGPT for general brainstorming and research. Use DebateLab for structured, repeatable debate practice that mirrors what you do in a round.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
