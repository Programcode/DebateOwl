A tiny PF reply block

This is an example resolution, not a claim about the current NSDA topic: "Resolved: Public schools should give students one homework-free weekend a month."

One argument, on the proposition. A weekend with no homework gives students time to sleep and to show up on Monday able to listen. The reason is ordinary: homework that fills both days crowds out the rest a student needs before a week of class. Why it matters: a tired first speech, in class or in a round, is a worse speech.

  1. If they say "Students will fall behind", we answer: one weekend a month is not the end of practice, and the other weekends still carry the load.
  2. If they say "Homework is how you learn", we answer: learning also needs a break, and a single protected weekend does not delete the assignments on the other days.
  3. If they say "Families will fill the time with more activities", we answer: that is the family's choice, and the school's job is not to occupy every hour so the choice never comes up.
