What a useful debate case includes

A strong case separates claims from warrants and impacts. It also makes weighing explicit so the judge knows why your argument matters more.

DebateLab helps turn a motion into contentions, reasoning chains, counters, and prep notes instead of a flat paragraph of generic arguments.

Use generated cases responsibly

Generated cases are drafts. The best workflow is to generate structure, check assumptions, add verified evidence, and then practice attacking the case yourself.

From draft to round strategy

Once the case exists, the real question is what the other side can exploit. Use DebateLab to find weak warrants, missing examples, and impact comparisons.

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.
