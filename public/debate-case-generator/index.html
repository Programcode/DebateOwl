What a useful debate case includes

A strong case separates claims from warrants and impacts. It also makes the weighing explicit so the judge knows why your argument matters more than the other side's.

DebateLab helps turn a motion into contentions, reasoning chains, counters, and prep notes instead of a flat paragraph of generic arguments.

How to use generated cases responsibly

Generated cases are a draft, not a substitute for research. The best workflow is to generate structure, check the assumptions, add verified evidence, and then practice attacking the case yourself.

This is why DebateLab links case generation to source finding, rebuttal practice, and flowcharts.

From case draft to round strategy

Once the case exists, the real question is what the other side can exploit. Use DebateLab to find weak warrants, missing examples, and impact comparisons before the round.

Frequently asked questions

Does DebateLab write the whole case for me?

It helps generate a structured draft, then you should edit, verify, and adapt it.

Can I choose a side?

Yes. Case prep starts from the motion and the side you are preparing.

Does it include evidence?

DebateLab can help find evidence leads, but you should always verify original sources.
