Should a teacher use DebateOwl or SchoolAI for debate?

For a debate club getting ready for a competition, pick DebateOwl: each student gets a case with "If they say… we answer…" replies and timed rounds in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum, and its free timer, motion generator and flow sheet need no accounts at all. For a whole class working with AI while you watch every chat, pick SchoolAI: it is a general classroom platform, not a debate product, with a free teacher plan of 5 Space sessions a year. Below are five needs, each with a pick.

Five things a debate teacher needs

Written for a debate coach or a class teacher choosing for this term, as of October 2026. SchoolAI facts come from its own site and help centre; DebateOwl facts from its app.

Getting a debate running tomorrow

DebateOwl

Put the free debate timer on the projector, draw a motion with the motion generator, and print a flow sheet for each student. Nobody signs up and nothing is installed.

SchoolAI

Sign up free with Google, Microsoft, Clever, ClassLink or email, build a Space and launch it. Students enter its code at schoolai.com/join or scan a QR code, and can join only while it is running. S8 S3 S4

Pick: DebateOwl's free tools, when the lesson is a live debate in the room.

Student accounts

DebateOwl

The free tools need none. For the app, each student signs up for their own account; there is no class roster and no way to add a class at once. It is for students aged 13 and older.

SchoolAI

Students join a running Space by code or link, and rostering through your school's systems comes with paid plans. S3 S6

Pick: SchoolAI, if every student in a class needs to be in one place without separate sign-ups.

Seeing what each student does

DebateOwl

No teacher view. Each student's cases and rounds stay in that student's account, and the free timer keeps the round in the browser, not on a server.

SchoolAI

Teachers see every conversation in real time, and Mission Control shows who is engaged and who is struggling. Reviewing student chats, down to a single chat, is on the free plan. S1 S2 S5

Pick: SchoolAI. This is the job it was built for.

Cost per student

DebateOwl

The free tools cost nothing for the whole class. The app is $5 a month per student after a 3-day trial, so a club of six is $30 a month plus tax. DebateOwl lists no school plan.

SchoolAI

Free for teachers, capped at 5 Space sessions a year. Pro and Scale are sold only to schools and districts, and the pricing page shows no dollar figures, so the cost per student is whatever your school agrees. S5 S6

Pick: SchoolAI's free plan for a few AI lessons a year; DebateOwl for a handful of competitors practising every week.

Debate formats

DebateOwl

The free timer has presets for Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, British Parliamentary, World Schools, Asian Parliamentary and the School 4-6-6-3 format, and lets you build your own. The app runs timed practice rounds in three of them: School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum.

SchoolAI

Its product pages name no competitive debate format. A help article gives debates as one use of its time-based activity tool. S9

Pick: DebateOwl.

Student data, in one paragraph each

SchoolAI lists SOC 2 Type 2, FERPA, COPPA and GDPR compliance on its trust page, and says it does not use student data to train AI models. A standard data processing agreement is available on every plan; custom changes to it come with paid plans. S7 S5

DebateOwl's free tools ask for no sign-up, so there are no student accounts to manage. The app is for users 13 and older and keeps each student's work behind that student's own sign-in.

A plain plan for a debate class

  1. This week: run a live debate with DebateOwl's free tools. No accounts.
  2. If you want every student talking to an AI under your eye: try SchoolAI's free plan, and save its 5 sessions for the lessons that need them.
  3. When the club has a competition: have the competitors start DebateOwl's 3-day trial and prepare the real motion.

Coaches also ask

Does SchoolAI have debate templates?

Its product pages we read describe none. Teachers build their own Spaces, so a debate activity is one you write, and a help article lists debates as an example for its time-based activity tool.

Can I see my students' DebateOwl work?

Not from a dashboard; DebateOwl has none. A student can export a case to Google Docs or PDF and share it with you.

Is DebateOwl free for a school?

The timer, motion generator, flow sheet and speech-time calculator are free, for any number of students, with no accounts. The app itself is paid per student after the trial.
