Should I practise on Debatable or prepare in DebateOwl?

Open Debatable when you want to argue out loud right now: pick a topic and a side, an AI opponent or a real person argues back by voice, and an AI judge scores the round out of 100 and says why. Open DebateOwl when the motion and the round date are set: it writes your case in speaking order with "If they say… we answer…" replies, finds the other side's weak points and then times a practice round. Thirty minutes in, Debatable has given you a spoken round and a verdict; DebateOwl, a case you can stand up and read.

Same thirty minutes, opposite order

Debatable starts with the round and leaves the writing out. DebateOwl starts with the writing and puts the round after it. Neither order is wrong; they suit different nights. Below, one evening runs on both, clock in the left margin. The times are a plan, not a stopwatch test, and each step describes what the product's own pages and screens offer as of November 2026.

One evening, minute by minute

  1. 0:00

    Debatable

    Open the AI voice page. Choose Casual back-and-forth, where you can interrupt, or Conversation, where the AI asks one question at a time. Pick a subject or write your own statement, take a side, pick the AI's voice. D2

    DebateOwl

    Start a Current Debate: the motion your coach gave you, your side, the format and your speaker role. Everything you make tonight is filed under it.

  2. 0:03

    Debatable

    You are already talking. The AI argues the other side aloud at the challenge level you chose (Relaxed, Balanced or Tough), and in Casual mode it may cut in like a person would. D2

    DebateOwl

    The case generator writes your case in the order you will say it: an opening, two to four arguments, each with an explanation, evidence naming a source and year, and why it matters, then "If they say… we answer…" replies and a closing.

  3. 0:15

    Debatable

    The round ends and the decision arrives within seconds: who won, a score out of 100 for each side, and reasons that quote what was said. On the free plan, one round like this uses about all of the 10 free AI voice minutes. D1 D3

    DebateOwl

    Attack reads the case the other side is likely to run and points at its weakest link. Factoids looks up a claim you are unsure of and suggests evidence with a named source to check.

  4. 0:25

    Debatable

    Rematch, switch sides or take a new topic, on a paid plan or with extra rounds bought. Or press Match to debate a stranger live, camera on, as an open conversation or as six timed speeches in 18 minutes. D4

    DebateOwl

    Crossfire questions you on your own case, one question at a time, so the replies you just read become answers you have already given once.

What you are holding at 0:30

From Debatable

One or more spoken rounds, each with a judge's decision, a score out of 100 per side and a reason for decision you can share or save. Practice in thinking on your feet against something that answers back. No written case, because writing one is not what the voice round is for.

From DebateOwl

A case for your side that you can read out, replies ready for what the other side will say, the weak spots in their likely case, evidence leads to open and check, and a set of cross-examination answers you have said once. Not yet a full timed round: in Public Forum that is the next 26 minutes of speeches.

Later the same evening

In DebateOwl, Sparring runs the round at your format's real speech times in School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas or Public Forum. You can type or talk, and in a voice round the AI opponent talks back. The round closes with a judge's verdict, a mark for each category and the next thing to work on; the judge reads your words, not your tone.

In Debatable, a live round against a person is the thing DebateOwl cannot give you. Its judging is built to be checked: three AI models from three companies judge each round, two matching votes decide it, the scoring rules are published before you speak, and either side can appeal to a human reviewer within 72 hours. Where Debatable is the better pick

  • You want a human opponent. Get matched with someone who disagrees, or post a challenge, and debate on camera. D4
  • You debate in a language other than English, Spanish or Hungarian. Its language page lists French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Mandarin, Japanese and Korean among others, with the AI opponent and the ballot in that language. Hungarian is not on the list. D5
  • You like a ladder. Rated live rounds move you on a public leaderboard that works like a chess rating.
  • Budget is tight and you mostly want voice practice. $10 a year buys 120 AI voice minutes every month. D3

Debatable, quickly

Is Debatable the same as itsdebatable.com and debateai.com?

Yes. As of November 2026 both addresses forward to debatable.com, which is where every fact on this page comes from.

Is Debatable free?

It has a free plan: live rounds with people after sign-in, and 10 AI voice minutes, about one round, that do not refill. Individual is $10 a year for 120 AI voice minutes a month, and Voice is $12 a month for the same allowance.

Can DebateOwl match me with a real person?

No. Every DebateOwl opponent is the AI. If arguing with strangers is the point, Debatable does that and DebateOwl does not.
