What AI does well

AI generates structure fast: case outlines, rebuttal blocks, topic brainstorming, and evidence leads. It saves time on the repetitive parts of prep.

What traditional prep does better

Original source reading, real coaching feedback, tournament-specific strategy, and adapting to a specific opponent all require human judgment.

How to combine them

Use AI for scaffolding, use traditional research for depth, and use coaching for strategy. DebateLab supports the AI scaffolding step.

Frequently asked questions

Is DebateLab an AI debate practice platform?

Yes. DebateLab focuses on debate prep workflows such as cases, rebuttals, evidence, flowcharts, coaching, and timing.

Can Google index private DebateLab app pages?

No. Private dashboards and account routes should remain noindexed while public guides provide crawlable context.

Should students verify AI-generated debate evidence?

Yes. AI can suggest research leads, but students should check original sources before using evidence in round.
