Prepared or impromptu: two different jobs

World Schools mixes two kinds of motion. Prepared motions are released weeks before the tournament, and impromptu motions come with one hour of preparation. The World Schools guide covers the rules; this page is about what AI is good for in each.

Prepared motion: weeks ahead

Here a tool can do real work. DebateOwl writes the case as a speech, an opening, two to four arguments with evidence that names its source and year, and the replies, so three speakers can split it. Attack then reads it as the opposition would and points to the weakest link. Factoids suggests evidence leads, each with a source to open and check before it goes near a speech.

Then read it aloud on the free World Schools timer. Eight minutes is longer than most school speeches, and the knock at 1:00 marks the point after which the other team may offer points of information.

Impromptu motion: one hour

The hour is the skill, so practise it as an hour. Draw a "This House" motion from the free motion generator and time the hour on the free timer, which takes custom prep periods of up to 30 minutes, so run two in a row:

  1. 10 minutes on what the motion means and what each side must show.
  2. 20 minutes building the team line and splitting material across three speakers.
  3. 20 minutes writing speeches.
  4. 10 minutes on the reply: which two or three clashes it will name.

Afterwards, run the same motion through Brainstorm and compare. Every angle it found that you missed is something to look for next time.

World Schools tool questions

Is debatelab.ai the same as DebateOwl?

No. DebateOwl was called DebateLab until October 2026, but debatelab.ai is a different product made by a different team, and DebateOwl has no connection to it.

Can DebateOwl run a World Schools round?

Not yet. Sparring runs School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum. For World Schools, use the case and reply tools on a prepared motion and the free timer for the speeches.

Who gives the World Schools reply speech?

The first or second speaker of each team, never the third. Opposition replies first, the replies take no POIs, and each is four minutes.

How should I use AI for an impromptu motion?

To practise the hour, not to replace it. Many tournaments restrict devices during impromptu prep, so the skill has to be yours on the day.
