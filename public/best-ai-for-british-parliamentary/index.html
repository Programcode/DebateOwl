Four benches, four different jobs

British Parliamentary puts four teams of two in one room: two on Government, two on Opposition, and each team competes against all three others, not just the bench opposite. The rules and speaker roles are on British Parliamentary debate. What each team needs from a tool is different, so here they are one at a time.

Opening Government

PM and DPM

The job: Define the motion and set a model the room can debate.

The trap: A definition so narrow that the opposition has nothing to say.

With DebateOwl: Write the case with the format set to British Parliamentary and your position named in the Instructions. Regenerate a weak section without touching the rest.

Opening Opposition

LO and DLO

The job: Clash with the model directly and set the opposition's line.

The trap: Opposing a version of the motion that Government never proposed.

With DebateOwl: Paste the government case you expect into Attack, which points to the link most worth hitting first.

Closing Government

MG and GW

The job: Agree with opening, then add an extension opening did not make.

The trap: Repeating opening's arguments with new examples and calling it an extension.

With DebateOwl: Brainstorm lists angles for the motion. Strike the two opening would obviously run and build from what is left.

Closing Opposition

MO and OW

The job: Out-extend Closing Government and, in the whip, sum up the debate by its clashes.

The trap: A whip speech that lists every speaker instead of the two or three questions that decided the room.

With DebateOwl: Brainstorm on the opposition side for the extension; the free flow sheet has a column for each of the eight speakers.

A 15-minute routine to practise before the tournament

BP teams get 15 minutes after the motion is released. Draw a "This House" motion from the free motion generator and run this against the clock, alone or with your partner:

  1. Minutes 0 to 2. Read the motion and your position. Write one sentence on what your team must prove that the other three teams will not.
  2. Minutes 2 to 6. Brainstorm angles for your bench. If you are closing, cross out what opening will say first.
  3. Minutes 6 to 12. Generate or write the case, then split it: who says what, and in which minute.
  4. Minutes 12 to 15. Write three POIs you will offer and the one-line answer to the POI you fear most.

Then give the first speech on the free BP timer. It knocks at 1:00 when POIs open and at 6:00 when they close, so you learn to have your roadmap and first argument out before anyone can stand.

British Parliamentary tool questions

Can DebateOwl spar a British Parliamentary round?
No. Sparring runs School 4-6-6-3, Lincoln-Douglas and Public Forum. A BP round has eight speakers on four teams, and DebateOwl does not play the other three teams yet.
When can I offer a POI in BP?
Between the first and the last minute of each 7-minute speech. Under the WUDC manual the protected time is 0:00 to 1:00 and 6:00 to 7:00, and judges disregard anything after 7:15.
Is the BP timer free?
Yes. The debate timer's BP preset runs the eight speeches, shows the protected time, knocks when POIs open and close, and needs no account.
Can I use AI in the 15 minutes of prep?
Only if your tournament allows devices in prep, and many restrict them. Use the routine on this page in the week before, so the habit is yours on the day.
